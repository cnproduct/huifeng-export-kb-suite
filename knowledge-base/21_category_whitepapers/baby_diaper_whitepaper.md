### 1. Executive Summary & Sourcing Abstract

### 3. Comprehensive Material Anatomy & Engineering Layering
#### Layer: Topsheet (Contact)
- **Engineering Specification:** 3D Pearl Embossed Hot-Air Through Nonwoven
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Huifeng Miaoyuan / Fiberweb
- **Functional Role:** Instant liquid diversion into channels, minimizes skin contact area by 40%

#### Layer: Acquisition Distribution Layer (ADL)
- **Engineering Specification:** Sub-layer High-Loft Airlaid Green/White Nonwoven
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Texbond / Jofo
- **Functional Role:** Rapid horizontal diffusion preventing localized pooling

#### Layer: Absorbent Core
- **Engineering Specification:** 7-Layer Composite Core (Sumitomo SAP + Virgin Fluff Pulp)
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Sumitomo Seika (Japan) / Georgia-Pacific (USA)
- **Functional Role:** Locks moisture into stable hydrogel; zero breakage after 500 mechanical shakes

#### Layer: Backsheet (Barrier)
- **Engineering Specification:** Cloth-like Breathable PE Microporous Film
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Tredegar / Huifeng In-House
- **Functional Role:** Permeable to vapor (water molecules cannot pass; air circulates freely)

#### Layer: Fastening System
- **Engineering Specification:** 3M Refastenable S-Cut Magic Velcro Tapes + Frontal Loop
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** 3M / Avery Dennison
- **Functional Role:** Withstands >50 refastening cycles with curved non-scratch edges

#### Layer: Side Leak Barriers
- **Engineering Specification:** Double Hydrophobic Nonwoven with Lycra Elastic Strands
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Invista Lycra
- **Functional Role:** 35mm standing barriers conform to baby groin preventing side leakage
