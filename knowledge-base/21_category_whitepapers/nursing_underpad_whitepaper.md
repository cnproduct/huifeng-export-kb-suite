### 3. Comprehensive Material Anatomy & Engineering Layering
#### Layer: Top Layer
- **Engineering Specification:** Medical-Grade Spunbond Nonwoven Fabric
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Certified Medical Nonwoven
- **Functional Role:** Tear-resistant when patient shifts weight; gentle on skin

#### Layer: Embossing
- **Engineering Specification:** Deep Diamond Capillary Grid Channels
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Precision Steel Roller
- **Functional Role:** Accelerates 360-degree liquid dispersion across full pad area

#### Layer: Core
- **Engineering Specification:** FSC Fluff Pulp + High Gel-Strength SAP
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Georgia-Pacific + Sumitomo
- **Functional Role:** Traps fluid securely and prevents wet pulp displacement

#### Layer: Backsheet
- **Engineering Specification:** Virgin Waterproof PE Film (Anti-Skid)
- **Tier-1 Supplier / Origin:** Huifeng Film Extrusion
- **Functional Role:** Prevents slipping on bed sheets and blocks 100% moisture penetration
